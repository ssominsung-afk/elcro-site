BUILT FOR SEAT ASSEMBLY

From the foam mold to the finished trim.

ELCRO develops automotive hook-and-loop components around the complete seat-assembly process. The backing integrates with expanding PU foam while the hook face creates a repeatable connection to loop material sewn into the seat cover.

Reliable performance

Automotive material construction engineered for secure foam integration.

ABOUT ELCRO

Specialized manufacturing from Ulsan, South Korea.

Founded in 2017, ELCRO focuses on professional hook-and-loop fastening systems for automotive seating and interior applications. Its manufacturing scope covers forming, coating, lamination, slitting and precision die cutting.
