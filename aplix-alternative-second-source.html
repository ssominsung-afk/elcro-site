Evaluating an APLIX alternative for a second source

If you are researching an APLIX alternative for automotive seat fastening, ELCRO can receive an application inquiry for technical review. Whether any ELCRO component is suitable must be established for the specific part and seat program. This page provides an evaluation process, not a product cross-reference.

Begin with the approved specification

Identify the incumbent part number, drawing revision, material specification, channel geometry and mating loop. Share only documents you are authorized to disclose. A similar appearance, nominal width or generic hook type does not establish equivalence. No APLIX-to-ELCRO interchangeability, drop-in fit, equivalent performance or customer approval is asserted.

Build a part-specific comparison

Review areaEvidence needed
Geometry and materialsControlled drawings, thickness, profile, radii, layer stack and agreed dimensional inspection.
Hook and loop pairingThe proposed pairing tested under the same conditioning, specimen size, loading direction and measurement method.
Foam integrationRepresentative molding trials, contamination inspection and retention in the intended seat pad.
Assembly and durabilityTrim-cover fit, appearance, handling and the environmental or repeated-engagement tests required by the customer.
Approval and supplyCustomer sign-off, required quality documents, sample approval, quotation and agreed delivery plan.

Use a staged approval process

  1. Agree the comparison requirements and document gaps before selecting a candidate.
  2. Review the covered and non-cover constructions against the actual process.
  3. Test candidate samples and the existing reference under the agreed plan. Record deviations rather than assuming equivalence.
  4. Complete the purchasing customer’s approval process before a production change. PPAP or other submissions must be explicitly agreed and supported.

What ELCRO needs for an inquiry

Send the application, authorized drawing, mating loop, estimated annual volume, target schedule and required approvals. See the quality evidence guide for document status. Pricing, lead time, capacity and tooling remain subject to a specific quotation.
